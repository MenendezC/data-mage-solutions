Custom SQL datasets

When the answer lives in three different tables, I join them.

BrightGauge's built-in datasets cover the common metrics. The questions behind margin and client retention usually need custom SQL. That's where I spend most of my time. Questions like:

  • Which clients went over their agreement hours this month?
  • What's our first-response time by priority and by tech?
  • Which agreements lose money once labor cost is counted?
  • How many tickets reopen within seven days of closing?
agreement_overage.sql 
SELECT c.company_name,
       a.agreement_name,
       a.included_hours,
       SUM(te.hours_billed) AS hours_used,
       SUM(te.hours_billed) - a.included_hours AS overage
FROM   time_entries te
JOIN   agreements a ON a.agreement_id = te.agreement_id
JOIN   companies  c ON c.company_id   = a.company_id
WHERE  te.date_worked >= DATE_TRUNC('month', CURRENT_DATE)
GROUP BY c.company_name, a.agreement_name, a.included_hours
HAVING SUM(te.hours_billed) > a.included_hours
ORDER BY overage DESC;
